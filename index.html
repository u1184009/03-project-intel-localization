Sustainability Through the Ages

Explore Intel's journey through time, discovering how our commitment to innovation has shaped a more sustainable future for technology and our planet.

Intel Sustainability Timeline

1968

Intel Founded

Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore rename the newly formed company NM Electronics to Intel Corporation, laying the foundation for decades of technological innovation.

1971

First Microprocessor

Intel debuts the 4004, the world's first commercial microprocessor, igniting the microprocessor revolution.

1978

8086 Processor

Launch of the 8086 processor, establishing the x86 architecture.

1985

386 Processor

Intel introduces the 386 processor with 32-bit architecture.

2006

Peak GHG Emissions

Intel reaches its highest annual greenhouse gas emissions and begins major investments in reduction programs.

2020

RISE Strategy

2022

Net-Zero by 2040

Intel commits to achieving net-zero Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions by 2040.

2023

Renewable Electricity

Intel achieves 99% renewable electricity usage globally.

2024

Sustainability Summit

Intel hosts its first Sustainability Summit.
